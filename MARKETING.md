# GoHonu — 7-Day Instagram → Bookings Plan

**Goal:** turn Instagram from "pretty but informational" into a funnel that drives clicks to
gohonu.com and books Hawaii tours. The money is in the **click**, so every post earns its
place only if it moves someone toward the link.

## The 3 rules that fix the old problem
1. **Every post ends with ONE clear CTA** → "Full list + book (free cancellation) 👉 link in bio"
   or "Comment **OAHU** and I'll DM you the link."
2. **Reels for reach, Stories for clicks.** Post 1 Reel/day + 3–5 Stories/day. Only Stories &
   bio can link — so use the **link sticker** in Stories every single day, pointing to a specific
   gohonu.com page (not just the homepage).
3. **Value first, sell second.** Teach/entertain for 90%, CTA for 10%. "Here are the 5 best Oahu
   snorkel tours — I ranked them by real Google reviews. Book any in 60 sec, link in bio."

## One-time setup (do before Day 1 — ~1 hour)
- **Switch to a Business/Creator account** (free) → unlocks insights + link stickers.
- **Rewrite bio:** `🐢 Hawaii tours rated by real travelers` / `Top Oahu adventures, booked in 60s` /
  `Free cancellation • 👇 gohonu.com`
- **Bio link:** point straight to **gohonu.com** (or /islands/oahu/). Optional: a link-in-bio page.
- **Story Highlights:** Oahu Tours · Deals · Flights · Where to Stay · Reviews.
- **Set up comment→DM** (ManyChat free tier): keyword "OAHU" auto-DMs the link. Huge for conversions.
- **Fix the old problem at the source:** make sure the IG account is NOT age-restricted (it was).

## The 7 days
Each day = 1 Reel + Stories. Hook in first 1 second. Trending audio. Geotag Waikiki/Honolulu.

| Day | Theme | Reel idea (hook → content → CTA) | Drive clicks to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Reintroduce + wow | "POV: you're snorkeling with turtles in Oahu 🐢" → 5 fast clips → "All bookable, free cancel. Link in bio." | `/islands/oahu/` |
| **2** | Hero experience | "This is the #1 rated adventure on the Big Island" (Manta Ray night snorkel) → why it's unreal → CTA | that tour page |
| **3** | Ranked list (saves!) | "Top 5 Oahu tours, ranked by real Google reviews ⭐" carousel/Reel → tease #1 → "Full ranking + book, link in bio" | `/islands/oahu/` |
| **4** | Deal + urgency | "Flights to Honolulu from $253 right now ✈️" → how to find them → "Search dates, link in bio" | `/flights/` |
| **5** | Social proof | "I only list tours real travelers rate 4.8★+ — here's proof" (show ratings) → trust → CTA | top-rated tour |
| **6** | Plan-my-trip | "How to do Oahu in 5 days (tours + where to stay)" → mini itinerary → "Full plan + book, link in bio" | `/islands/oahu/` + `/stay/` |
| **7** | Engage + recap | "You asked — here are the turtle tours 🐢" (reply to comments as a Reel) → recap week → strong CTA | best performer |

## Daily non-negotiables (15–20 min)
- Post the Reel at a peak time (test 7–9am & 5–7pm HST; your audience is mainland US pre-trip).
- 3–5 Stories with a **link sticker** to that day's page. Use polls/questions ("Snorkel or shark dive?").
- Reply to every comment + DM within the hour (IG rewards it; it also closes bookings).
- Spend 10 min commenting on #oahu #hawaii #thingstodoinoahu posts (visibility).

## What to measure (weekly)
- IG: reach, **saves**, **shares**, profile taps, **link clicks** (bio + story stickers).
- Site → bookings: check FareHarbor (asn-ref), Viator (pid P00111627), Travelpayouts (marker 541388),
  Stay22 (aid gohonu) dashboards. Link clicks that don't convert = fix the landing page; low clicks =
  fix the hook/CTA.

## Beyond 7 days (compounding)
- **Lead magnet:** "Free Oahu 5-day itinerary" (PDF) → collect emails → email new deals weekly.
- **Repurpose** every Reel to TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Pinterest (Pinterest is huge for trip planning).
- **Collab** with 1–2 micro Hawaii creators/month (gift a tour for a Reel).
- Turn your best site content (top-rated lists, deals) into a repeatable weekly series.
